# Security review of `main` for production (#541)

Board #553 (DAY-PLAN-19, 2026-11-01). Reviewed commit: `99d357c` and every PR merged on the board after it
(listed in section 9). Reviewers: [K] (backend, infrastructure, CI) and [O] (apps and the public surface).

## How this review was done

- **Read against:** `SECURITY.md`, `docs/27` (authorization), `docs/31` (health data), `docs/35` (white label),
  `docs/37` (the public surface), `docs/runbooks/production-readiness.md`, and OWASP ASVS 4.0.3 level 2.
- **Allowed:** reading code, running the unit and integration tests, local containers (`docker-compose.local.yml`),
  `pnpm audit`, `dotnet list package --vulnerable`, and `cdk synth` with cdk-nag.
- **Not done (`SECURITY.md`):** no scanner, fuzzer or load against the shared dev environment, and no read of real
  dev data. The OWASP ZAP scan of a production-like stage is #542.
- **Severity:** critical (another studio's data, someone else's account, or a secret, with no precondition), high
  (the same with a precondition a real attacker meets, or health data), medium (a weaker control, a defence in depth
  gap, or personal data in a place it shouldn't be), low (hardening and hygiene).
- **Outcome per finding:** a PR (critical and high are fixed on the board) or an issue labelled `security` with a
  default. Accepted risks name who accepts them.

## 1. Tenancy and row-level security [K]

Every tenant table has RLS enabled and forced; every policy keys on `app.organization_id`; the app role can't bypass
it; `SECURITY DEFINER` functions pin `search_path` and return only what their caller may see; `SetTenantAsync`
moves only after a check; the platform role's reach.

## 2. Authorization on every route [K]

Every endpoint against `docs/27`'s permission matrix: route group, permission key, self-only checks (`/me/*`),
object-level checks (an id from another studio in the body or path), step-up where `docs/27` asks for it, the
operator pool and `OperatorRouteFilter`, suspended studios and disabled accounts.

## 3. Tokens and sessions [K]

JWT validation (issuer, audience or client id, token use, lifetime, algorithm, JWKS), the two schemes kept apart,
just-in-time provisioning, `auth_time` and step-up, the branded sign-in (`docs/36`).

## 4. Health data [K]

The data key (SSM, version, never overwritten), AES-256-GCM use (nonce, associated data), the audited read, who may
read answers, export, erase and merge, the withheld answers when the key can't be read, retention.

## 5. The public surface: routes and limiters [K + O]

Public studio pages, join and share links, the og:image and story routes, invitations and join codes, attempt
limiters, what an anonymous caller learns (enumeration), CORS, request size limits, WAF rules.

## 6. Background work: worker, queue, webhooks, scheduler [K]

Message types and who may put them on the queue, poison messages and the DLQ, the scheduler's targets, push
subscriptions and VAPID keys, the retention and tripwire jobs, the Neon usage function.

## 7. Infrastructure, secrets and IAM [K]

Least privilege per Lambda role, SSM parameter scopes, the GitHub OIDC role (#123 is Kris's decision and is
recorded, not reopened), the media bucket, logging and log retention, cdk-nag suppressions and their reasons, #122.

## 8. CI and the supply chain [K]

Workflow permissions, pinned actions, `pull_request_target` and fork PRs on a public repo, secrets in Actions logs,
gitleaks and Semgrep coverage, pnpm `minimumReleaseAge` and `blockExoticSubdeps`, NuGet lock files, dependency
audits.

## 9. Logs, errors and personal data [K]

What reaches CloudWatch (no emails, names, tokens or health data), Problem Details bodies, the audit log's content,
correlation ids.

## 10. The apps [O]

CSP and headers as served by both Pages sites (`infrastructure/lib/web/pages-headers.ts`), `_worker.js`, the
sign-in callback and every redirect or `next` parameter, `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` and the service worker
cache, the white-label inputs shown to other people, the `no-raw-html` test's reach, `pnpm audit` for both apps.
